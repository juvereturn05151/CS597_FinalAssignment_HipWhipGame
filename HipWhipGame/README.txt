Build instructions: 
1. Open the Unity
2. Click "Build on Switch"

Required Equipment:
1. 2 Magical Belts
2. 4 Straps that you can attach the right Joy-Con to a belt

Equipping Instruction:
1. Your left arm holds the left Joy-Con.
2. Put the 2 straps on a belt
3. Attach your right Joy-Con to these straps.

Controls:
1. Stretch your left arm to punch.
2. Swing your left arm to grab.
3. Jump to do a homing attack.
4. Wiggle your butt to do a butt attack.
5. L to block.
6. ZL to activate special move

Individual Focus: everything.

Additional Notes: This game needs to be played with 2 players.
